E menu — your shark in 3D, its stats, and the + buttons that spend points
Everything you eat pays points: a fry 1, a lunker 5, a dolphin 40, a whale 150. Spend them on health, attack, attack speed or stamina — upgrades are expensive, so pick a direction rather than a bit of everything
Boost burns the green ring beside you — empty it and you swim at cruise until it refills
Move the mouse to look · collect the glowing orbs
Hunt to grow: fish go down in one bite, a dolphin takes 10 and runs from you — a whale takes 50, and hits for half your health
Eating heals you — the bar over your head shows your health whenever it moves
Whales fight back, and early on they win. Bite one and it turns on you — it rears back before every strike, so swim clear and come back in. Break off for 10s and it forgets you. Come back for it once you have spent some points
The NEAREST readout points you at the nearest big animal
