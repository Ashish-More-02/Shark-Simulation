Controls

Six keys and the mouse are the whole input surface. WASD swims and the mouse looks; hold Shift to burn the stamina ring for speed, and left click to bite — everything you eat pays points, and E is where you spend them. Whales fight back and early on they win, so pick your fights. Esc frees the cursor whenever you want out.
